PageFly is out. Microsoft Clarity replaces it. Clarity is free and rated 4.6 from 2,229 reviews on the Shopify App Store. It gives you session recordings, heatmaps and funnel drop-offs. Keep its AI shopping chat (Brand Agents) switched off, because it would talk to customers in your name. Landing pages get built as theme templates with the Shopify CLI you already use.
Once the store passes about 1,000 sessions a week, add Intelligems (4.7 stars, 14-day trial) for A/B tests. Content and page tests start at $69/month; discount, free-gift and bundle tests at $112.10/month. Below that traffic, a test takes too long to reach a result.

Research engine: treg
treg is the agency's source of live market data, and most calls cost a tenth of a cent to 1.5 cents. The team balance is $0.97, which covers roughly 300 to 900 research calls.
First test, Sep 24, 2 calls for $0.002 total. On TikTok Shop, SAVILAND's cat-eye gel pen has sold 20,932 units at $17.99, yet it's rated only 3.7 stars from 2,537 reviews. MrWish sells a single pen at $49.58 and has sold 27. Buyers want this product, the best seller has a quality problem, and a single pen at a premium price barely sells. BlushTip's opening is sets at a fair price, a better experience, and honest reviews.

Custom agents, built Sep 24 (not in the catalog)
Creator Seeding Manager. Finds 20 to 50 nail and beauty micro-creators each month, drafts outreach, tracks gifted units, codes and posts.
Reviews and UGC Manager. Asks for photo reviews 10 days after delivery, gets permission to reuse, and feeds the best photos to ads and the product page.

Built Sep 24: Sourcing Scout. It searches AliExpress and Alibaba in camofox, on a logged-out profile, for the cheapest reliable supplier and shipping for the pen and the lamp. It ranks them by landed cost per unit (item, shipping and duties), then compares against your the owner's agent quote. It's read-only and never messages a supplier or places an order. No catalog agent does this, and treg has no AliExpress or Alibaba data. Since Aug 29, 2025, US imports no longer enter duty-free under $800, so the Scout flags every price that doesn't include duties.
Skip the catalog's China-market agents (Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Taobao, WeChat). BlushTip sells in the US and 27 other countries, not in China.

Retention engine
Welcome flow, 3 emails: quiz result and shade pick, a 3-step how-to, then real customer photos once you have them.
Post-purchase flow: how-to on delivery day, curing tips on day 3, a review request on day 10, removal how-to on day 14, a new-shade suggestion on day 25.
Shade of the Month: one limited seasonal shade each month, for example a fall brown cat-eye in October. Email and SMS subscribers get 48 hours early access.
Shade Club subscription: 2 new shades a month for about $24, skip or cancel any time, via the free Shopify Subscriptions app. This one is new for the category.
Give $10, get $10 referrals, plus a small reward for photo reviews. Photos feed the product page and ads.
Discount rules
Prefer value over percent-off: a free lamp, a free magnet wand, or buy 2 pens, get a 3rd free.
One sitewide sale a year, for Black Friday to Cyber Monday (Nov 27 to Nov 30, 2026). Constant discounts teach people to wait.
Urgency only when it's true: a real stock count, a real end date. No fake timers.
